# Stage E2 benchmark (not authored yet)

This directory will hold the Stage E2 evaluation: reader contracts, fixtures, questions, calculation sheets and gold
answers (design: [docs/stage-e/stage-e2-evaluation-design.md](../../docs/stage-e/stage-e2-evaluation-design.md);
decisions: [docs/stage-e/stage-e2-decisions.md](../../docs/stage-e/stage-e2-decisions.md)).

**No fixture, question, calculation sheet or gold answer is written yet.** Authoring starts only after the owner
approves the authoring protocol and the contracts are merged (LABS-1.064 review gate).

What is here now:
- `spec/reader-contracts.md`: the **final** E2 reader contracts (E2-D3): proposals approved under LABS-1.063 (§0.10)
  and all 25 semantic decisions approved under LABS-1.064 (§0.11). At review before merging. Decision record:
  [stage-e2-semantic-decisions.md](../../docs/stage-e/stage-e2-semantic-decisions.md).
- `spec/blocked.json`: the one decision still open (ONT-SC-1) and the approved-but-unimplemented answer paths, which
  scored questions may not expect (G probes only).
- `spec/coverage-matrix.md`: the proposed corpus allocation and coverage (LABS-1.064, for review).
- `spec/authoring-protocol.md`: the proposed authoring and isolation protocol (LABS-1.064, for review). E2 is
  development-tier: procedurally isolated, not externally independent or sealed.
- `spec/reader-docs.md`: the documentation strings of every reader the E2 harness can call, generated by
  `evaluation/e2/reader_docs.py` (GAP-HARNESS-4). These are the readers' own descriptions of themselves. They are not
  given to question authors (protocol §3).

The harness that will run E2 is `evaluation/e2/`. Its review is
[docs/stage-e/stage-e2-harness-review.md](../../docs/stage-e/stage-e2-harness-review.md). Its tests use only the synthetic
self-test fixtures in `tests/fixtures/e2/` (`harness-synthetic.json`, `evidence-roles.json`, `decisions.json`), which are
not E2 evaluation fixtures.
